How It Works

Three simple steps from discovery to payment.

1

Discover

Tell the AI what you need and your budget in plain language. Voice or text — your choice.

2

Compare

Our AI finds the best matches, compares products by price and features, and explains why each is a good pick.

3

Confirm & Pay

Review the exact amount, confirm you approve, then complete your payment securely through PayPal Sandbox.

Your Security is Non-Negotiable

  • Every payment requires your explicit confirmation before checkout
  • PayPal secrets are never exposed to the browser
  • All amounts are verified server-side — frontend values are never trusted
  • Sandbox mode only — no real money is ever transferred
  • Voice data is processed locally in your browser — not stored or transmitted
